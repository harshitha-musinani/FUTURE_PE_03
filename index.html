SEO Content Cluster Project

AI-Powered SEO Blog Strategy for a Local Business using Prompt Engineering, content clusters, local SEO, and keyword-focused blog planning.

1. Objective

The purpose of this project is to create a structured prompt workflow that generates SEO-optimized blogs and content clusters for business websites.

  • Generate SEO blog outlines from H1 to H3
  • Write long-form useful blog content
  • Create pillar and supporting blogs
  • Use local SEO keywords naturally
  • Support leads, trust, and authority

2. Business Chosen

Business Type: Dental Clinic

Business Name: SmileCare Dental Clinic

Location: Bangalore

This project is designed as if the content will go live on a real clinic website. The goal is to help the clinic rank higher on Google and attract local patients.

3. Primary Keyword & Search Intent

Primary Keyword: Best Dental Clinic in Bangalore

Search Intent: Users want to find a trusted dental clinic, compare services, understand treatment costs, and choose the right dentist.

This keyword targets local users who are ready to book or seriously consider treatment.

4. Pillar Blog Topic

Title: Best Dental Clinic in Bangalore – Services, Cost & What to Expect

This is the main pillar blog designed to build authority, answer important questions, and connect supporting blogs under one topic cluster.

5. SEO Prompt Framework

Reusable Prompt:

You are an SEO content writer. Create a long-form SEO blog for [BUSINESS TYPE] in [CITY]. Primary Keyword: [KEYWORD]. Target Audience: [AUDIENCE].

Include SEO title, meta description, H1-H3 headings, internal linking suggestions, helpful human-readable content, and a strong business-focused conclusion.

8. Internal Linking Strategy

The pillar blog links to all supporting blogs. Each supporting blog links back to the main pillar article.

This strengthens topical relevance and helps search engines understand the website structure better.

9. Local SEO Strategy

The city name is naturally included throughout the content to target local search traffic.
